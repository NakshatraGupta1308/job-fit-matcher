Senior Backend Engineer, Data Platform

What You'll Do

- Improve the performance and reliability of our PostgreSQL-backed services
- Partner with product and data science to scope and ship new features
- Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Experience designing RESTful APIs and microservices
- Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production
- Experience with workflow orchestration tools such as Airflow
- Solid understanding of monitoring and observability practices

Nice to Have

- Familiarity with Terraform and infrastructure as code
- Experience with GCP or AWS
